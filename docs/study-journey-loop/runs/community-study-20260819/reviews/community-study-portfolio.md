## Review Findings

1. The ten posts differ by information structure, not only title or color: process checkpoints, safety route, team roles, clue trail, observation board, interview boundary, overall/detail pairing, lab tasks, day chapters and viewpoint verification.
2. Every post has at least three local images and a stage list. Longer posts use headings and bullets to remain scannable.
3. Uncertainty is handled by omission or an explicit question in the post itself; visible “事实与待确认” boilerplate is not used.
4. Four collections map to real sample IDs and report sample counts consistently.
5. The implementation reuses existing Compose surfaces and does not add decorative social-media chrome.
