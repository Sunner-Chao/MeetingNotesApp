# Community Domestic Study-Tour Portfolio Candidate

## Purpose

Provide offline sample activity pages that feel like domestic travel notes with learning inside them. The reader should immediately know where the journey goes, what scenery or site is being visited, what to notice there and what small understanding to carry home.

## Content Contract

- Lead with a domestic destination, scenic feature, museum, historic street, village or route.
- Organize the body by real journey stops, not by industrial process steps.
- At each stop, combine direct experience, guide explanation and one small learning question.
- Keep a compact takeaway block, such as a learning card, route memory or photo arrangement tip.
- Do not invent opening times, fees, reservations, rankings, weather, identities, precise itinerary promises or unsupported historical conclusions.
- Bind every bundled image to the local attribution registry.
- Keep the sample boundary visible until a non-empty live response replaces it.

## Visual Contract

- Reuse `community-featured-carousel-v1` and `community-sample-live-bridge-v1`.
- Prefer a scenic overview as the first image, then details, path context and a learning-card or return-view image.
- Allow up to seven media items for a multi-day post; remove duplicate landscape frames.
- Keep Microsoft/Fluent blue-gray tokens, stable image bounds and a single reading hierarchy.
- Keep Xiaohongshu as structure research only; do not ship its screenshots, copy, chrome, stickers, fonts or brand marks.

## Representative Fixture

The integrated sample set covers West Lake, Liangzhu, Suzhou, Zhangjiajie, Huangshan, Guilin, Sanxingdui, Xi'an, Wuyuan, Qinghai Lake and a four-day multi-destination route. It covers scenic observation, museum clues, cultural travel, village observation and multi-day chapters.

## Validation

`MockStudyCommunityDataTest` checks post count, domestic destination markers, scenic/travel/learning tags, long copy, image existence, attribution coverage, collection resolution, multi-day filtering and banned industry-report drift.
