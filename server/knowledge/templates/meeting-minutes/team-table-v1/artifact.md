## Page System

- One portrait Letter section (`8.5 x 11 in`).
- Margins: `0.87 in` on all sides.
- Header/footer distance: `0.5 in`.
- No first-page or odd/even header variation.

## Typography

- Title: `16 pt`, bold.
- Primary numbered headings: `13 pt`, bold.
- Secondary numbered headings: `11.5 pt`, bold.
- Body and table text inherit the document Normal style.

## Content Flow

1. Meeting theme.
2. Core conclusion summary.
3. Numbered discussion topics.
4. Consensus table.
5. Unresolved-issue table.
6. Action tracking table.
7. Pending confirmations.
8. Research and reserve items (backlog) table.
9. Risk table.
10. Meeting conclusion.
11. Meeting images with captions when supplied.

## Rendering Rules

- Preserve the five source table patterns and repeat their header rows across pages.
- Expand rows vertically; do not shrink body text to fit long content.
- Keep image aspect ratio, constrain images to the printable width, and place captions directly below each image.
- RAG supplies authorized facts only. The template registry owns this layout and its slot schema.
